Smart ad detection

Spots ads no list knows about, the way a person would: "Ad" and "Sponsored" labels, AdChoices icons, standard banner sizes, and boxes served by other companies. It learns as you browse.

  • Ads it finds on a site are remembered and hidden instantly on your next visit.
  • When the same ad company shows up on 3 different sites, it is blocked everywhere.
  • Everything is learned and stored only on this computer.
