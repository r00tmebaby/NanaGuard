# Privacy Policy – Hush

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

Hush does **not** collect, store remotely, sell or share any personal data.

- **No tracking, analytics or telemetry.** Hush makes no network requests of its own.
- **Browsing data stays local.** Hush inspects pages only inside your browser to block popups and scam tricks. Page content and URLs are never sent anywhere.
- **What is stored, and where:**
  - Your settings (on/off and allowed sites) are kept in the browser's extension storage and may sync between your own devices through your browser account (Chrome Sync / Firefox Sync).
  - Block counters are kept in local extension storage on your device only.
- **Permissions:** "Access to all websites" is required so Hush can protect every page you visit. `declarativeNetRequest` blocks known popup ad networks, `scripting` injects the protection script, `tabs` closes ad tabs and shows per-tab counts, and `storage` saves your settings.

Uninstalling Hush deletes all of its stored data.
